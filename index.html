Blackspire

A hundred floors, each held by a boss. Find the chamber, kill what is inside, climb.

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. You attack the way you face: J or a click to strike, K or Space for your skill, Shift to roll, and hold H to block if you carry a shield. Esc pauses. On a phone, drag the left side of the screen.
