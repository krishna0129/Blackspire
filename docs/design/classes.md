# Class balancing

Status: **decided, not built yet.** "High", "normal" and "low" are relative; the numbers come from playing and the
balance runner. Builds on [combat.md](combat.md) (mana, stamina, dash, parry) and the classes in
[skills.md](skills.md#classes-and-boards). Part of the road to the first friends' playtest ([roadmap](../roadmap.md)).

## Decided

### Roles in a party of four

| Class | Role |
|---|---|
| Marauder | tank, area damage |
| Duelist | melee damage, parries |
| Ranger | ranged damage, slows |
| Shadow | single-target damage, mobility |
| Witch | area damage, control, minions |
| Templar | healer and support |

### Healing

- **The Templar is the healer.** Anyone can heal a little through gear (healer sets and pieces), but at most about
  **10% of what a well-built Templar heals** (one in a healing set, with a faith weapon).

### Base numbers

| Class | Health | Mana (max and regen) | Stamina | Dash | Blocks / parries |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Marauder | high | medium | high | normal | parries with a greatsword; blocks and parries with mace and shield |
| Duelist | normal | low | high | normal | parries with a longsword; blocks with the buckler |
| Ranger | normal | medium | normal | long | no |
| Shadow | low | low | high | longest | **parries with daggers** |
| Witch | low | high | low | short | no |
| Templar | normal | high | low | short | blocks and parries with mace and shield |

### Classes that can't block or parry

- The **Ranger** and the **Witch** defend themselves by **dexterity** (evade) and **the dash**: putting points into
  dexterity and dodging. No defensive skill is given to make up for it. Against a boss's must-parry attack they dash
  clear ([bosses.md](bosses.md)).

### Starting attributes

- A new character starts with a **small lean toward its class** (a few points already in its main attributes), and
  places the rest freely. The amounts are to be decided in detail.
