# Balance

Generated by `npm run balance` (tools/balance.js) from the game's own rules: do not edit. What the scenes are,
and how builds are made, is at the top of tools/balance.js; why, in [the skills design](design/skills.md#the-balance-runner).

Until the new combat sets a reference damage per floor, each build's damage is held against the **median of the builds on
that floor**. ⚠ marks a build more than 20% from it. Health lost is in the build's own health bars (2.0 = twice its
health), and falls count how often it died and was stood up again.
